School calendars, view only

These show for everyone as view only. Nothing here can change them. Keep adding and editing events in Google Calendar or SignUpGenius as usual, and they appear here within about ten minutes.

Where to find a Google calendar's address
  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar.
  2. In the left column, point at the calendar's name, choose the three dots, then Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
  4. Paste it into the address box here and choose Connect.

Anyone holding a secret address can read that calendar, so it is kept on the server and never shown to educators. If your Google account does not offer a secret address, make the calendar public and paste the Public address in iCal format instead.

Showing SignUpGenius sign ups, such as conferences
  1. In SignUpGenius, open the Sign Ups page, choose Tools on the left, then Calendar Subscriptions. This needs a paid SignUpGenius plan.
  2. Choose Create iCal Feed, give it a title, and add the conference sign ups, up to five.
  3. Choose whether to include filled slots, unfilled slots, or both, then Save.
  4. On the feed, choose the calendar icon, then Calendar Help, and copy the webcal link.
  5. Here, choose Add a calendar, name it, paste the link in its address box, and choose Connect.

Families keep signing up in SignUpGenius. Fulton Calendar only shows what is there.
